CASTLE: It is one  of the best public domain games  we've  ever  come across.
        You  move  this little guy around a  castle  picking  up things. 
        You are trapped in a deserted Castle and you must escape. It is rumored
        that the  castle is  full of treasures.  Can you find them all?  

Use the cursor keypad to move your man around the rooms.  To pick up a visible 
item,  just run into it.  To pick up an item not displayed  on  the screen, use
the command  GET.  To  attack  monsters just  run  into  them,  but only if you
have a weapon.The computer accepts most two word commands. There are 83 rooms &
13 treasures.  To  see the High Score , look at the wall in the courtyard. 

LOOK AT EVERYTHING CAREFULLY!           To save a game type : SAVE + Filename 
                                        To load a game type : LOAD + Filename 
                                        To End the game type: QUIT
  
 You can also use  many words such as LOOK, FIND, OPEN, .....   but, alwayse
 in a two words sentense as: FIND KEY or LOOK DESK or OPEN DOOR or .......
