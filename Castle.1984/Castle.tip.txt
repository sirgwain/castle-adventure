These are some tips and cheats for the CASTLE game. You'll 
probably enjoy the game more if you DON'T read this file... use 
it if you get stuck.





























Commands...

INVENTORY:	Shows what you are carrying.
GET:		Pick up something.
DROP:		Loose sopmething.
WAVE:		The wand.
READ:		The book.
PLAY:		Harp.
LOOK:		See what something is.

The things you can find are:

Hourglass	A treasure.
Helmet		Protects you when fighting monsters.
Sword		Essential when fighting monsters.
Cross		Zaps vampire. (SHOW CROSS)
Harp		Zaps fairie. (PLAY HARP)
Jade Figurine	A treasure behind vampire.
Diamond		A treasure behind fairie.
Wand		Zaps magician's mirror & king's passage.
Crystal Ball	Behind magician's mirror. (LOOK BALL) shows where scepter is.
Necklace	Treasure hidden on statue of king. Protects from traps.
Gem		Hidden in fountain.
Lamp		In garden. Needed in dark places (kitchen and king's passage.)
Key		On desk in king's study. Needed for locked doors to dungeon.
Eyeglasses	On desk in guest room. Needed to read book.
Rubys		Treasure
Wine Flask	On desk in cook's room.
Gold Bar	Treasure in maze.
Goblet		Treasure.
Scepter		In secret room in winding passage. Get into dungeon with the
		key,the necklace, the wand and the lamp. Two frames into the
		passage WAVE WAND. A passage appears. Go in and get the 
		scepter. Go back to the courtyard and wave the scepter at
		the gate to escape.

Monsters:

Ogres, Deamons, Bat, Spiders, Snake. 
		Kill 'em by ramming them and keeping them rammed 'til they
		snuff it. Wear helmet and sword or they'll snuff you.
Vampire, Fairie
		Block passages.
